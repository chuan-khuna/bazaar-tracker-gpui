# Combat keywords

Sources: Mobalytics, "The Bazaar Keywords Explained" (<https://mobalytics.gg/the-bazaar/guides/keywords-and-terms>), read 2026-11-09. The game patches often: when a card's text disagrees with this, trust the card.

## Damage over time

- **Burn**: deals damage twice a second, and drops by 1 each time it does. Shield halves Burn damage; Heal cleanses 10% of it.
- **Poison**: deals damage every second. Shield doesn't block it; Heal cleanses 10% of it.

## Defence and sustain

- **Shield**: blocks damage. Poison ignores it, and it halves Burn damage.
- **Heal**: restores Health, up to Max Health, and cleanses 10% of Poison and Burn.
- **Regen**: restores Health equal to your Regen every second.
- **Lifesteal**: when the item deals damage, you recover that much Health.

## Speed and control

- **Haste**: the item charges twice as fast.
- **Slow**: the item charges half as fast.
- **Freeze**: the item doesn't charge and can't activate.
- **Charge**: immediately advances an item's cooldown.
- **Flying**: Slow and Freeze applied to the item last half as long.

## Item effects

- **Crit Chance**: the chance for an item with a cooldown to crit, which doubles its Damage, Heal, Shield, Regen, Burn and Poison.
- **Multicast**: each use, the item's effect happens that many times.
- **Ammo**: an item with no Ammo left stops charging. Ammo refills after each fight.
- **Destroy**: a Destroyed item is fully disabled: it doesn't activate, and its passives stop.
- **Repair**: one of your Destroyed items works again.
- **Transform**: the item becomes a random item of the same size from your hero's pool (even when the item was neutral or another hero's), keeping its enchantment if it had one.
- **Upgrade**: raises the item's tier, which makes it stronger and worth more.
- **Quest**: the item gains more abilities once a condition is met, often buying a number of a kind of item or reaching a stat in combat.

## Hero mechanics

- **Heated and Chilled** (Jules): items and Board slots can be Heated or Chilled; items whose text mentions it get extra effects.
- **Notes C, D, E, F, G, A, B** (The Dragons): a slot mechanic; each Note gives 10% cooldown reduction to its type of item.
- **Tempo** (The Dragons): each second of combat makes Tempo, which items spend for bonus effects. Some items make more Tempo, or count the Tempo gained over the whole fight.
- **Rage** (Karnok, from the Karnok's Rage skill he starts every Run with): using items builds Rage. At 100 he Enrages: Slow and Freeze are cleared from his items, and their cooldowns are 10% shorter for 5 seconds.
